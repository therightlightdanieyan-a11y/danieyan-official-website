ABOUT THE ARTIST

Born Sylive Buwote Jonathan in 2005 in Magamaga, Mayuge District, Uganda, Thanrwicho Danieyan TK is professionally known as Golden Voice Bway.

He is a versatile musician, recording artist, songwriter, composer and online poetry writer. His creative interests span different musical genres, allowing him to explore diverse sounds and expressions.

He writes his own lyrics and melodies and also composes music for other artists.

MY MUSICAL JOURNEY

Early Childhood

My interest in music began around Primary Three, inspired by international artists and their musical creativity.

2019 — Songwriting

While studying at Namagabi High School, I had written more than 76 complete original song lyrics.

2020 — Development

I continued learning and improving my musical flows through guidance and creative experiences.

2023 — New Connections

I met producers, writers and other music creatives in Masajja Kibira, along Salama Road.

2024 — Recognition

My songwriting and composition abilities began receiving recognition among some upcoming artists.

2026 — Nairobi

I moved to Nairobi, Kenya, to pursue my music career and began my professional recording journey.
